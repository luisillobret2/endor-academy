Positioning Summary

💡 The One-Liner

Checkmarx is a SAST incumbent with very broad language coverage and a deep query engine — don't try to out-language them, out-signal them. Endor Labs keeps only reachable findings in the backlog (up to 97% SCA noise reduction reported by Zebra Technologies), AI SAST agents cut up to 95% of false positives without query tuning, and Endor Patches backport fixes when no upgrade exists. Be candid that Endor Labs SAST is newer; contrast on tuning burden, reachability, and time-to-value.

Why Teams Choose Endor Labs

Noise reduction as a design principle

Function-level call graphs across 40+ languages mean only vulnerabilities the code actually calls reach the backlog — up to 97% SCA noise reduction reported by Zebra Technologies. Astronomer calls Endor Labs “noise-canceling headphones for vulnerability management.”

AI SAST instead of query maintenance

Security-engineer agents trace dataflow across every repo and PR and cut up to 95% of false positives — no query tuning, no dedicated triage staff.

Remediation that closes the loop

Governance for the AI coding era

Modern deployment without the services bill

Watch Out

⚠️ Where Checkmarx is Genuinely Strong

Checkmarx's engine is genuinely deep — very broad language coverage, a flexible query language, and a long enterprise track record. Teams with dedicated AppSec staff who live in queries get real value from it, and the install base is large.

Acknowledge it, then pivot: “For teams that need to write their own queries, Checkmarx is a serious engine — the question is what it costs your engineers to keep it precise.” Contrast on false-positive burden, reachability, and remediation. Be candid that Endor Labs SAST launched in Aug 2025; lead with SCA noise reduction and AI SAST precision, where the evidence is strongest.

Competitor Background

Objection-Handling Reminder

⚠️ Keep It Classy

Never disparage Checkmarx by name in front of a customer. Contrast on outcomes — false-positive burden, time-to-fix, and total cost of ownership — and let the buyer draw their own conclusion. If query depth comes up, acknowledge it and steer to signal quality: up to 95% fewer false positives with AI SAST.
