Positioning Summary

💡 The One-Liner

Veracode is a trusted enterprise SAST veteran — don't fight two decades of compliance credibility, join it. Frame Endor Labs as the reachability-first, AI-native platform for teams whose bottleneck is triage and time-to-value: only vulnerabilities the code actually calls reach the backlog, AI SAST agents cut up to 95% of false positives, and Egnyte cut MTTR by 70% while accelerating FedRAMP. Be candid that Endor Labs SAST is newer (launched Aug 2025); pivot to speed-to-value, developer flow, and agentic coverage Veracode doesn't have.

Watch Out

⚠️ Where Veracode is Genuinely Strong

Veracode's rule depth, compliance pipelines, and audit credibility are real — in regulated industries, their name on a report carries weight, and their policy engines are mature. Security leadership may already trust the brand.

Acknowledge it, then pivot: “Veracode is a strong compliance gate — the question is what your developers experience every day.” Contrast on time-to-value, reachability, and AI-era coverage. Be candid: Endor Labs SAST launched in Aug 2025 and its rule depth is still growing; position rule-based SAST plus AI SAST as the modern complement, not a like-for-like swap on day one.
