Why Deal Registration Matters

Deal registration is the foundation of your economics as an Endor Labs partner. Developing an opportunity takes weeks of discovery, demos, and proof-of-value work — and registration is how you make that investment count. A registered deal gives you margin protection on the opportunity, so the value you create translates into the value you keep.

Registration also keeps everything orderly for the customer. Once a deal is registered, the Endor Labs channel team knows you own the relationship, routes any inbound contact from that account to you, and brings sales engineering and executive support to your deal instead of around it. First-to-register wins — an approved registration takes priority if another partner surfaces the same opportunity.

Finally, registered deals are how tier advancement is earned. The partner program (Module 07) rewards consistent registered revenue and certification; nothing advances your tier like a pipeline of well-managed registrations.

💡 Register Early

The moment an opportunity has a named customer contact, an identified pain, and a plausible timeline, it belongs in the partner portal — not in your notebook. Early registration protects your margin, unlocks support, and prevents channel conflict before it starts.

The Registration Process, Step by Step

The process is deliberately simple — the discipline is in doing it every time, and keeping it current.

  1. Qualify first. Confirm you have a named contact, a real pain (noise, MTTR, AI governance, compliance), and a timeline a buyer has actually stated — not one you hope for.
  2. Submit in the partner portal. Capture the customer, the opportunity size and products in scope, the incumbent or competitor, your value-add (services, integration, training), and the technical shape: developers, languages, repos, CI/CD systems.
  3. Validation. The Endor Labs channel team checks for conflicts with other partners and direct-sales activity. First-to-register takes priority, so earlier is strictly better.
  4. Approval. An approved registration locks in your margin protection and deal-specific support for the registration window.
  5. Keep it current. Update stage, amount, and close date as the deal moves. Registrations that go stale can lapse — a two-minute update protects weeks of work.

Register expansions and renewals too. A new business unit adopting the platform, an add-on SKU like Endor Patches or AI SAST, and a co-termed renewal are all registerable moments — and each one protects the economics of work you are already doing.

Pipeline Hygiene That Wins

A healthy pipeline is the difference between a predictable quarter and an anxious one. These habits are simple, and almost nobody does all of them consistently:

  • Coverage of 3x. Keep registered pipeline at roughly three times your quarterly target; deals slip, and coverage is what absorbs the slippage.
  • Honest stages. A deal is in the stage the buyer’s action has earned, not the stage that makes the quarter look better. Inflated stages waste everyone’s forecast.
  • Buyer-driven close dates. Date the close by when the buyer’s procurement actually moves — then plan backwards for security review, legal, and paper.
  • Multi-thread every deal. Engage security, engineering, and procurement in parallel. Single-threaded deals die with one vacation or one reorg.
  • Review weekly. Look at registered deals weekly — which moved, which stalled, and what evidence the next stage needs.

Stage discipline is where most pipelines quietly rot. Use this reference to keep every registered deal honestly staged:

Deal Stage Evidence That Earns It Common Mistake
Qualified Named contact, stated pain, and a timeline the buyer gave you Counting a good conversation as a deal
Demo delivered The prospect confirmed the pain, and the next step is on calendars Leaving the demo with no scheduled follow-up
POV in flight Written success criteria, a named owner, and a fixed window An open-ended “evaluation” with no criteria
Verbal commit The economic buyer is engaged and the paper process is known Single-threading on a friendly champion with no budget authority

⚠️ Numbers Come From the Portal

Registration amounts, margins, and program rates live in the partner portal — never quote pricing to a customer from memory or estimation. When a prospect asks for figures mid-process, commit to a date for a confirmed quote rather than a number you cannot stand behind.

Co-Selling with Endor Labs

You own the customer relationship. Endor Labs brings resources that make your deal stronger: sales engineers for competitive demos and POV support, product expertise for technical deep-dives, and executive sponsorship for strategic opportunities. On registered deals, those resources come to you — use them.

Co-selling works best when it is planned rather than reactive. Loop in your channel manager early on competitive deals so the SE is booked before the demo, not after it. Present POV readouts together so the customer sees one team. And keep the portal current as the deal moves so support, escalation, and pricing actions are never waiting on paperwork.

The division of labor that works: you run discovery, qualification, and the relationship; Endor Labs provides product depth, SE firepower, and the platform roadmap; together you deliver the proof of value and the readout. Done well, the customer experiences a single, confident team — and your margin protection does its job at signature.
