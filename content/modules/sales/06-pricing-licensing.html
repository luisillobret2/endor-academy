How Endor Labs Is Licensed

Endor Labs is sold as a quote-based subscription built on a per-seat scan-credit model: customers buy seats and scan credits sized to their engineering organization and scan volume, so cost scales with the protection they actually use rather than with repo counts or lines of code. This is the model to explain first in any commercial conversation — it aligns what customers pay with the value they consume.

The platform is licensed through tiered SKUs — for example Core and Pro tiers — with capability add-ons such as Endor Patches (backported fixes for Java and Python), AI SAST (agentic code review on every pull request), and the SBOM hub. This structure lets a customer start with the scope that matches their priority and expand as they prove value: SCA reachability first, AI SAST and SBOM governance next, Endor Patches where legacy dependencies make upgrades hard.

The Free Developer Tier and the Commercial Platform

One part of the portfolio is deliberately free: the developer tier for AURI and the MCP tools. Developers can pull Endor Labs into their existing environment at no cost — the MCP server (npx -y endorctl ai-tools mcp-server) runs in Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, and IntelliJ, and AURI inventories and governs coding agents, models, MCP servers, and skills. No procurement cycle, no budget conversation, no excuse not to start.

Use that free tier as your land motion. A developer-facing adoption of agent governance and workflow integrations creates pull from the people who fix code; the commercial platform — reachability analysis, SAST, secrets, containers, Package Firewall, policies, SBOM/VEX — is where the value concentrates and where the subscription lives. The free tier earns trust; the platform monetizes it.

💡 Two Motions, One Platform

Free developer tier = the adoption motion (get Endor Labs into the tools developers already use). Commercial subscription = the value motion (reachability, remediation, firewall, policies, SBOM across the organization). Lead with the first, sell the second.

The TCO Conversation

Sticker price is the wrong battlefield. The right one is the total cost of the noise: the engineer-hours a team spends triaging findings that were never a risk, the SLA misses that erode security’s credibility, and the audit hours spent explaining unremediated criticals. Reachability attacks that cost directly — Zebra Technologies cut SCA noise by 97%.

Tool consolidation is the second lever. Many prospects run separate SCA, SAST, secrets, and container tools with separate price tags, consoles, and policies. Endor Labs unifies them — one system from finding to fix — so the comparison is one subscription against four line items. Finally, remediation economics close the case: Egnyte improved mean time to remediation by 70%, which is engineering capacity returned to the roadmap.

Cost Lever Question to Ask Endor Labs Impact
Triage labor “How many engineer-hours a week does vulnerability triage consume?” Reachability cuts the backlog to what the code actually calls — Zebra cut SCA noise 97%
Slow remediation “How long from finding to merged fix today?” AI remediation, automated PRs, and Endor Patches — Egnyte reached 70% faster MTTR
Tool sprawl “How many AppSec consoles and contracts are you managing?” SCA, SAST, secrets, containers, SBOM/VEX, and policies in one system
Supply chain incidents “What would one malicious package in your build cost?” Package Firewall blocks malicious packages at install time, before dev machines or CI

Handling Pricing Questions in the Field

Pricing questions are buying signals — answer them with process, never with invented numbers. These rules keep every commercial conversation credible:

  • Never quote unconfirmed figures. Rates, margins, and deal specifics come from the partner portal. A number you invent becomes the anchor you cannot defend.
  • Qualify the shape first. Developers, languages, repos, scan volume, and the SKUs in scope determine the quote — nail those down before requesting one.
  • Frame the model, not the price. Explain the per-seat scan-credit subscription and tiered SKUs; the model itself answers “will this scale with us?”
  • Use the free tier to answer budget objections. The developer AURI/MCP tier costs nothing — momentum can start this week while the subscription is sized properly.
  • Bring multi-year conversations to the channel manager. Terms, co-terming, and growth allowances are negotiated deliberately, with deal registration in place (Module 05).

⚠️ The Portal Is the Single Source of Truth

Every price you discuss with a customer should be one you can trace to the partner portal. If a prospect presses for a figure on the spot, give them a date for a confirmed quote instead — “I’ll have exact numbers to you by Thursday” builds more trust than a guess ever will.
