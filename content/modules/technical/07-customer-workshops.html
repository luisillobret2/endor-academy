The 90-Minute Workshop Agenda

A workshop is not a demo with a longer calendar invite. The customer’s engineers do the work on their own repository, and the session ends with a decision. Ninety minutes is enough if the agenda protects the hands-on blocks and cuts the slide time.

Time Segment Output
0–10 min Intro: who is in the room, what we will decide, and the one outcome that matters Agreed objective written on screen
10–30 min Live scan of a repository they chose, connected through the SCM app with toolchain detection building the scan profile Their code scanned, first findings visible
30–55 min Reachability triage exercise: small groups work the top findings and record call-path evidence Reachable versus unreachable list with reasons
55–75 min Remediation planning: upgrade impact analysis, an automated pull request, and an Endor Patch where no upstream fix exists Named owners and a first fix in flight
75–90 min AI and agent governance session, then decisions and next steps Agent policy decisions and a dated follow-up

💡 Pro Tip

Send the agenda and a pre-read one business day ahead, including which repository will be scanned and who needs access. Ask the customer to nominate a repository that is small enough to scan live and important enough that the results matter.

Running the Live Scan & Reachability Triage Exercise

The live scan segment is where trust is earned or lost. Prepare in advance, narrate what the platform is doing, and let the customer’s engineer drive the keyboard where possible.

  1. Confirm access before the session — SCM app installed, repository visible, and a scan profile that covers the languages in the room.
  2. Narrate toolchain detection — show how the platform identified the languages and generated the profile, so the result is explainable rather than magical.
  3. Split into pairs for triage — each pair takes three to five critical findings and decides reachable or unreachable using the call-path evidence.
  4. Capture dismissals in the product — record reasons as they are agreed, so the exercise produces a real triage state instead of a whiteboard note.
  5. Review the noise delta together — compare total findings against reachable findings and let the customer state the reduction themselves.

⚠️ Important for Partners

Never triage the customer’s findings for them. The moment you classify a finding without the call path, you become the tool. Hand over the evidence, ask which finding they would fix first, and let their engineers own the verdict.

The AI & Agent Governance Session

Close with the topic most customers have not governed yet. Run it as a short working session, not a feature tour: inventory first, policy second.

  • Inventory — list the agents, models, MCP servers, and skills in use. Ask who approved each one and where its context files such as AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md live.
  • Decide policy — agree which capabilities are allowed, blocked, or require a human decision, and who reviews ask-a-human requests.
  • Trace accountability — walk through the audit trail and Agents Hub trust and attribution so the customer can answer “which agent did this?”
  • Cover install-time risk — show Package Firewall blocking a malicious package so the group sees the control that acts before any scan.
  • Extend to the IDE — demonstrate the MCP server through npx -y endorctl ai-tools mcp-server in the editors the team already uses.

💡 Pro Tip

Ask the room a single question to open this block: “Who can tell me which agents have credentials to your repositories right now?” Silence is the gap statement, and it lands harder than any slide.

Facilitation Tips & Follow-Up Materials

Facilitation is a discipline of restraint: ask more than you tell, keep the clock visible, and write down decisions in the customer’s words.

  • Start with a question, not a slide. “What would make these ninety minutes worthwhile?” then shape the agenda live.
  • Protect the hands-on blocks. If time slips, cut your own narration, never the exercise.
  • Invite the quiet participants. Security, platform, and compliance attendees all need a moment to speak, or their constraints surface after the meeting as blockers.
  • Stay neutral about incumbents. Position Endor Labs as the consolidation path, not a verdict on the tool the room bought.
  • Capture visibly. Keep a shared document open and let participants watch their input become the action list.

Follow-Up Package

Send the summary within 24 hours: participants and objective, findings scanned, the reachable versus unreachable result, decisions made, action items with owners and dates, and the artifacts the session produced. Attach the SBOM and VEX export if compliance was in the room, and include the next meeting invite in the same message.

⚠️ Important for Partners

Do not let the workshop end without a dated next step. A workshop that closes with “we will circle back” costs the customer a day and costs you the momentum. Book the readout or the architecture review before anyone leaves the call.
