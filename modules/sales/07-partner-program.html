The Three Partner Tiers

The Endor Labs partner program has three ascending tiers — Silver, Gold, and Platinum — and this academy is its training arm. Tiers exist to reward partners who invest in competence: the deeper your certified bench, the richer the economics and support you unlock. Advancement is earned through certification and consistent registered revenue, and every benefit you care about — margins, leads, marketing support — scales with the tier.

🥈

Silver

The entry tier. Certified staff, completed baseline enablement, standard deal-registration margin, and partner portal access. Your starting point — and the foundation for advancement.

🥇

Gold

For partners with a proven record of registered deals and a certified sales and technical bench. Enhanced margins, lead distribution, and co-marketing participation.

💎

Platinum

The strategic tier for partners functioning as an extension of the Endor Labs go-to-market: premium economics, lead distribution priority, co-marketing investment, and a dedicated sales engineer.

💡 Benefits Scale With Certification, Not Just Revenue

The program deliberately weights advancement toward certified competence: trained partners position better, demo better, and deliver better. Certification is the lever entirely within your control — start pulling it today.

Benefits That Scale With Certification

Across the three tiers, five benefit categories grow as you advance:

  • Training and enablement. This academy — learning paths, hands-on labs, and certifications — is the program’s training backbone, and deeper tiers get deeper enablement access.
  • Deal-registration margins. Registered deals earn protected margin (Module 05); higher tiers earn better economics on the same work.
  • Lead distribution. Endor Labs routes qualified inbound demand to partners; higher tiers receive priority.
  • Co-marketing. Joint campaigns, events, and content programs that put your brand next to Endor Labs’.
  • Dedicated sales engineering. At the top tier, a named SE supports your demos, POVs, and technical delivery — the difference between borrowing resources and owning them.

Read the benefits as a flywheel: certification earns tier, tier earns margin and leads, margin and leads fund more certified staff. The partners who grow fastest never treat certification as a one-time hurdle — they keep their bench current as teams and products change.

How Academy Certifications Map to Tiers

The academy’s certification ladder is the program’s competency standard: Certified Associate → Certified Professional → Certified Expert, plus the partner-aligned Specialist credentials and the flagship Master Architect. Track XP and module completion feed directly into exam readiness — completing this Sales Enablement track is the substantive step toward the Partner Sales Specialist.

Certification What It Validates Requirements
Certified Associate Baseline fluency with the Endor Labs platform and the AppSec problem it solves Foundation-level learning across the academy
Certified Professional Deeper, multi-track competence across SCA, SAST, and the broader platform Progresses from Associate-level knowledge
Partner Sales Specialist Positioning, discovery, competitive play, and commercial process — this track Associate plus completing the Sales Enablement track
Partner Technical Specialist SE-level demos, POV management, and deployment depth Professional recommended, plus the Technical SE track
Certified Expert Advanced, cross-track mastery of the platform Professional first; advanced multi-track study
Master Architect The flagship credential — solution architecture at scale Expert plus Technical Specialist

For a partner building toward Gold, the practical sequence is: get your sellers through this track to Partner Sales Specialist, get your engineers to Partner Technical Specialist, and register every deal along the way. For Platinum, the bench typically includes Expert-level and Master Architect staff — partners who can lead architecture conversations, not just attend them.

Getting More From the Program

Tier requirements, current rates, and program specifics live in the partner portal — never quote them to customers from memory. What you control is the progression plan, and the partners who advance treat it like a sales plan:

  1. Certify early. Map your staff against the certification ladder and schedule the academy time now — it is the fastest lever you own.
  2. Register everything. Every qualified opportunity, expansion, and renewal goes into the portal (Module 05).
  3. Work the co-marketing. Propose joint activities with your channel manager; funded programs reward the partners who show up with plans.
  4. Use the SE you get. At the top tier, the dedicated sales engineer is a growth asset — put them in front of every competitive demo and POV.
  5. Review quarterly. Meet your channel manager on a cadence to check progress toward the next tier, not just the last quarter’s revenue.

⚠️ Don’t Promise What You Can’t Verify

Tier thresholds and program rates change; the partner portal is the source of truth. When a customer or your own leadership asks about program specifics, check the portal before you answer — credibility compounds, and so do errors.
